name: Muscle-up counter isolation
description: User's scope rule for future muscle-up repetition-classification changes.
---

Do not change how correct muscle-up repetitions are identified or counted when calibrating the incorrect-rep counter. A video may deliberately use incorrect form; use its logs to tune only the incorrect path.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires correct-rep counting to remain unchanged while incorrect reps are calibrated.

**How to apply:** Keep correct-top criteria and count updates out of scope for incorrect-rep tuning. Adjust only incorrect/no-top classification and verify that correct counts stay unchanged.
